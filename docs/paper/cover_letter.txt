Dear Editor,

We submit the manuscript "DriftJax: Structure-aware differentiable device
simulation" with the accompanying software (v0.1.17-post2, MIT licence,
archived on Zenodo under concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22762674) for
consideration as a Computer Physics Communications Program Library article.

What the paper contributes. DriftJax is a one-dimensional
drift-diffusion-Poisson simulator in JAX that supplies reverse-mode
sensitivities of scalar design objectives through an implicit-function
adjoint (custom VJP). The implementation combines an analytically
assembled 3x3 block-tridiagonal Jacobian with a pivoted banded forward
solve (LAPACK dgbsv) and a pivoted dense-LU adjoint, plus a layered
verification harness (analytic-vs-AD Jacobians, conservation identities,
manufactured solutions, finite-difference Taylor studies, cross-code
comparison). Example applications cover sensitivity ranking, bounded
band-gap/thickness optimisation, and synthetic IV-curve recovery.

Relationship to prior work. The model-to-gradient workflow builds
directly on Mann et al.'s partial-PV; the manuscript distinguishes
shared methodology (implicit differentiation, analytical derivatives)
from its own implementation choices (pivoted banded forward algebra,
documented backward stability strategy, verification record) and
withdraws unmatched historical timing claims in favour of controlled
single-architecture benchmarks.

Reproducibility. The repository pins exact dependencies
(requirements-pinned.txt), archives per-example JSON sidecars,
execution logs and figures (docs/paper/records/), the raw full-suite
test log (237 passed, 1 xfailed; tests/v017_test_log.txt), and the
machine-readable benchmark record. No measured-device validation is
claimed; physical validation against experiment is stated as future work.

Authorship and conflicts. Authors: Mohammed Benaissa (first and

Algiers). The authors declare no competing interests. The work is
original and not under consideration elsewhere.
